## Art direction (SPEC §31 + pilot lessons)
- **One picture = one fate.** A focal motif that says what happened (what the title and cause name), a setting matching the node's place, a mood for the kind: **good** warm/clear light, open space; **bad** cold or sick colour, closing-in composition, one wrong detail; **death** dark, heavy vignette, the player's silhouette or a marker left behind — aftermath, never gore.
- **No two endings alike.** Different focal composition, viewpoint, and where the bright mass sits (left/right/low/top); the duplicate gate compares an 8×8 luminance layout and a pixel-difference test, so avoid one glow-in-the-middle template. Not a recoloured scene: existing scenes may be a far background via `scene_bg(e, key, dim=0.3)` but add your own focal motif and lighting.
- **Claim endings** (4 gods × your route): use `GOD[god]["ramp"]` for colour so the gods stay consistent across routes; change composition and setting per route. Khorne: charge / line against a low red sun; Nurgle: growth, garden, flies, green-ochre; Tzeentch: a door, threads, pieces, blue/pink-violet; Slaanesh: mirror, lone figure, applause, magenta/silver.
- Luminance bands (route averages, good > bad > death): good ≈ 65–85, bad ≈ 38–58, death ≈ 20–42 (`endpreview.py` prints mean luma).
- **Taste / IP:** no text or lettering in the image, no GW marks/logos, no likeness of named canon characters (omens/archetypes only), no gore (aftermath, implied, shadows), no real-world symbols, no large literal sigils.
- **Technical tips (from the pilot):** backlit silhouettes need a bright backdrop (light the horizon band, not the whole sky — a black figure on a black plain turns into a wire outline); sprites are half-body busts with a flat bottom edge → hide it behind ground/haze, use `bottom_fade=`, or crop; xenos/daemons/enemy astartes face left, humans face right (mirror as needed); `rim()` only lights edges ≥ 3 px thick; don't outline everything; keep each picture to ~20–28 hand-picked colours and use `blend()`, `light_pool()`, `haze()` for light, avoid per-pixel random colours and big `mix()` loops, use `e.protect` for tiny bright accents; faces can't be drawn on figures — paint 2–3 px at `f["head"]`; use `player_fig(route, …)` so the same person reads across the route's endings; omens are framed square pictures — crop with `spr()` if you want them as props.
- Typical first-draft failures: dark figure on dark background, wrong scale, muddy focal point. Expect 3–6 render-and-look rounds per picture. View results with the Read tool on the PNG (`tools/art/endpreview.py <id…> --scale 6` writes `/tmp/endpreview.png`, which you open with Read).

## Files / ownership (concurrency: 8 art agents work at the same time)
- You own **only** `tools/art/endings_<your route>.py` (create it if missing, follow the pilot modules; the module is already listed in `MODULES` of `build_all.py` behind an exists-guard) and your route's PNGs in `assets/endings/`. **Do not edit `endkit.py`, `reg.py`, `build_all.py`, `assets/manifest.js` by hand, other routes' modules, `data/**`, `src/**`, other tools.** Need a helper? Write it in your module with a leading underscore (`_helmet`, `_grot`, …) and mention it in your report. Name each function after its ending id. Don't re-sort the brief.
- Render loop: `python3 tools/art/endpreview.py <id> --scale 6` while drafting; `python3 tools/art/build_all.py --endings --faction <route>` renders your route's PNGs and the contact sheet `tools/art/contact_endings_<route>.png` (open it with Read and compare against the route's other endings: all must look different and fit their kind). Other agents run the same command for their own route at the same time: it only writes that route's files (plus a manifest rewrite, which is deterministic and safe); if you ever see a half-written manifest or an error from another route's module, wait a minute and retry.
- Gate for your route (run at the end): `python3 tools/art/build_all.py --check-endings --partial -v` → no problems reported for your route (size 192×108, ≤ 32 colours, not near-empty, no near-duplicates, luminance order good > bad > death on your route's averages, manifest lists your PNGs). Other routes' missing PNGs are expected while the others are still working.
- Determinism: all randomness seeded from the ending id (`e.rng`, `e.noise`); a re-render must be byte-identical.
